TIER 3 & 4: Core Anti-Rotation, Upper Body & Auxiliary Arms Execute: Monday Gym Session
Core Anti-Rotation & Stability (4 Exercises)
Exercise Prescription Tempo / Cues Primary Target & Benefit
Side Planks w/ Top Leg Abduction Core 3 sets × 30–45s/side 30–45s isometric hold with a steady 1s lift/lower cadence for leg abductions. Oblique & hip abductor co-contraction for stride alignment.
Cable / Band Pallof Press Core 3 sets × 10–12 reps/side 2s push out, 2s isometric hold at full extension, 2s return, 0s rest. Anti-rotation stability against torso twisting under fatigue.
Copenhagen Adductor Side Planks Core 3 sets × 20–30s/side 20–30s static isometric hold maintaining rigid posture and top knee pressure. Groin & adductor resilience to prevent pelvic torque.
Single-Arm Suitcase Farmer's Carries Core 3 sets × 40–50 meters/side Continuous controlled walk pace with zero lateral torso lean. Deep core anti-lateral flexion & grip endurance.
Primary & Auxiliary Upper Body (5 Exercises)
Incline Dumbbell Bench Press Upper 3 sets × 8–10 reps 2-0-1-0 (2s lowering, 0s bottom pause, 1s press up, 0s lockout). Upper chest & anterior shoulder stability for uphill drive.
Chest-Supported Dumbbell Rows Upper 3 sets × 10–12 reps 2-1-1-1 (2s lowering, 1s bottom stretch, 1s pull, 1s top squeeze). Mid-back posture to prevent rounding over late in the race.
Parallel Bar Dips Upper 3 sets × 8–12 reps 2-0-1-0 (2s lowering to 90° elbows, 0s bottom pause, 1s push up, 0s top lockout). Lower chest, front delts, and tricep push power.
Overhead Rope Tricep Extensions Upper 3 sets × 12–15 reps 2-0-1-1 (2s lowering, 0s bottom pause, 1s extension up, 1s top squeeze). Long-head tricep endurance for backward arm swing.
Incline Dumbbell Bicep Curls Upper 3 sets × 10–12 reps 3-0-1-0 (3s lowering, 0s bottom pause, 1s curl up, 0s top pause). Bicep long-head strength for rhythmic forward arm drive.
TIER 1: Downhill Quad Armor & Eccentric Durability Execute: Wednesday Gym Session (Post-Easy Run)
Quad & Eccentric Focus (5 Exercises)
Exercise Prescription Tempo / Cues Primary Target & Benefit
Slow Eccentric Box Step-Downs Lower 3 sets × 8–10 reps/side 3-0-1-1 (3s controlled lowering, 0s bottom tap, 1s drive up, 1s top lockout). VMO & Patellar tendon durability for steep downhills.
Walking Dumbbell Lunges Lower 3 sets × 10–12 steps/side 2-1-1-0 (2s step down/lower, 1s bottom pause, 1s drive up, 0s step transition). Unilateral quad braking strength & hip deceleration.
Isometric Wall Sits w/ Single-Leg Extension Lower 3 sets × 45–60s hold 45–60s static isometric hold at a 90° knee angle with continuous wall pressure. Isometric fatigue resistance under continuous muscular tension.
Bulgarian Split Squats Lower 3 sets × 8–10 reps/leg 3-1-1-1 (3s lowering, 1s bottom pause, 1s drive up, 1s top lockout). Deep quad & glute hypertrophy with pelvic balance.
Goblet Front Squats Lower 3 sets × 10–12 reps 3-1-1-0 (3s lowering, 1s bottom pause, 1s drive up, 0s top rest). Anterior chain load tolerance & upright core stabilization.
TIER 2: Posterior Chain, Hips & Lower-Leg Stability Execute: Friday Gym Session
Posterior & Lower Leg Focus (6 Exercises)
Exercise Prescription Tempo / Cues Primary Target & Benefit
Single-Leg Dumbbell RDLs Lower 3 sets × 8–10 reps/leg 3-1-1-1 (3s hip hinge lowering, 1s bottom stretch, 1s drive up, 1s top squeeze). Hamstring lengthening under load & pelvic rotation control.
Single-Leg Glute Bridges Lower 3 sets × 12–15 reps/side 2-1-1-2 (2s lower, 1s bottom pause, 1s lift, 2s peak squeeze at the top). Gluteus maximus isolation & hip extension power.
Mini-Band Monster Walks & Clamshells Lower 3 sets × 15–20 reps/dir 1-1-1-0 (1s controlled step/abduction, 1s peak hold, 1s return under constant band tension). Gluteus medius conditioning to prevent knee valgus/collapse.
Straight-Knee Calf Raises (Gastroc Focus) Lower 3 sets × 12–15 reps 3-2-1-1 (3s lowering, 2s bottom stretch, 1s drive up, 1s top pause). Gastrocnemius tendon stiffness and explosive push-off power.
Bent-Knee Calf Raises (Soleus Focus) Lower 3 sets × 15–20 reps 3-2-1-1 (3s lowering with slight knee bend, 2s bottom stretch, 1s drive up, 1s top pause). Soleus endurance (absorbs up to 8x body weight) for late-race cramp prevention.
Tibialis Wall Raises Lower 2–3 sets × 15–20 reps 1-2-2-0 (1s pull up, 2s top hold, 2s lower, 0s bottom). Active dorsiflexion and shin splint prevention.
Swiss Ball / Machine Hamstring Curls Lower 3 sets × 10–12 reps 2-1-1-1 (2s extend legs, 1s bottom pause, 1s curl in, 1s top squeeze). Isolated hamstring contraction during late stride recovery.
